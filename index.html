    Formulas used
    • MAS loan cap: OMV ≤ S$20,000 → max loan 70% (min 30% down); OMV > S$20,000 or blank → max loan 60% (min 40% down).
    • Downpayment: S$ = Price × % ÷ 100, % = S$ ÷ Price × 100; changing price keeps the %.
    • Loan = Car Price − Downpayment
    • Tenure: n = Years × 12 + Months (1–84); years in formula = n ÷ 12
    • Total Interest = Loan × (Rate ÷ 100) × (n ÷ 12)
    • Monthly Instalment = (Loan + Total Interest) ÷ n
    • EIR (approx.) ≈ Flat Rate × 2 × n ÷ (n + 1)
    • Running costs / month = yearly items ÷ 12 + monthly items
    • Total Monthly Car Cost = Instalment + Running Costs
    • Total Cost of Ownership = Downpayment + Total Repayable + Running Costs × n
    • Minimum salary = Monthly Cost ÷ 0.70; Recommended = Monthly Cost ÷ 0.30; annual = ×12
    • Bar: ≤ 30% green (Comfortable) · 30–70% amber (Stretching) · > 70% red (Not affordable)

    Estimates only. Rates, MAS rules and costs change; check with your bank/dealer.
